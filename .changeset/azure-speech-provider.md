Studio: Azure Speech (Azure AI Speech) can be connected under Settings → Providers for voice-over with its neural voices. The key carries the resource's region (`<region>:<key>`, or `AZURE_SPEECH_KEY` with `AZURE_SPEECH_REGION`), and one without a region is refused where it is pasted; Azure times no words, so subtitles spread the text over the line, as with OpenAI's voices.
